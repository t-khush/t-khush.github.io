Personal sites have a useful kind of freedom. They don’t need a content strategy or a publishing cadence. They can just be a place to think in public, one note at a time.

That’s what I want this site to be.

I spend most of my working hours thinking about software systems: the layers that coordinate work, the boundaries that keep services dependable, and the tooling that helps engineers move faster without losing the plot. Outside work, I tend to bring the same curiosity to my homelab—usually with fewer users and considerably more willingness to break things.

I’ll use this space for notes on infrastructure, AI systems, and whatever I learn while building. There will probably be detours into self-hosting, movies, and New York food, too.

No grand manifesto. Just a place to leave useful breadcrumbs.
